# Product Taxonomy — Jometry / D'WALL Architectural Systems

## Overview

The D'WALL product architecture is built on a single unified platform logic: a 500 × 500 mm demountable panel grid attached via patented toolless mounting nodes to a concealed backer plate, creating a continuous, multi-functional service cavity behind the finished surface.

```
PRODUCT PLATFORM: D'WALL Modular Architectural Platform
  │
  ├── 01. D'WALL Service Wall (Production)
  │     ├── Service & Access Panel
  │     └── Hygiene Panel (Healthcare & Clean Areas)
  │
  ├── 02. D'WALL Acoustic Panel System (Production)
  │     ├── Slotted Acoustic Tile (Primary Absorber)
  │     ├── Solid Acoustic Tile (High-Hygiene / Unperforated)
  │     ├── Backlit Acoustic Tile (Integrated LED)
  │     └── Service & Access Acoustic Tile (Integrated Sockets & Outlets)
  │
  └── 03. D'WALL Living Wall System (In Development / Concept)
        ├── Living Planted Cassette (Biofilter & Foliage)
        ├── Microgreen Cassette (Edible Crop)
        ├── Preserved Moss Panel (Dry Decorative / Acoustic)
        └── Ventilation & Access Accent Panel (Anodised Bronze Micro-perforated)
```

## 2. Product Families

### Family 01 — D'WALL Service Wall
- **Status**: Production (3 commercial installations in Mumbai)
- **Primary Function**: Service-accessible partition and wall cladding concealing power, data, conduit, and trunking.
- **Key Characteristics**: No wet trades, no construction dust, repeatable access, integrated sockets in panel face.

### Family 02 — D'WALL Acoustic Panel System
- **Status**: Production (3 commercial installations in Mumbai)
- **Primary Function**: Tunable resonant cavity absorber with architectural rib aesthetic.
- **Key Characteristics**: Standoff depth determines targeted absorption spectrum (low, mid, high); 90° rotatable six-rib tiles create directional, basket-weave, or gradient patterns.

### Family 03 — D'WALL Living Wall System
- **Status**: System in Development / Concept (Prototypes in progress, testing planned)
- **Primary Function**: Modular demountable living wall treating botanical elements as an integrated building service.
- **Key Characteristics**: Off-site pre-cultivated cassette exchange, 4-layer internal leak defense, optional biofiltration air path.

## 4. Configuration, Finishes & Application Scenarios

### Standoff Depth Configuration
- **Shallow Cavity**: High-frequency acoustic control / power & data cabling only.
- **Medium Cavity**: Speech-band acoustic absorption / conduit & small trunking.
- **Deep Cavity**: Low-frequency acoustic control / large trunking & MEP services.

### Tile Layout Configurations (6-Rib Module)
- **Directional**: Uniform vertical or horizontal rib alignment.
- **Basket-Weave**: Alternating 90° tile rotation creating parquet grid effect.
- **Gradient**: Graduated slot density across wall elevation.

### Standard Finishes
- Matte Paint (Custom colours to specification)
- Wood-Effect
- Metallic
- Anodised Champagne-Bronze (Micro-perforated accent panels)
