# Catalogue Structure — Jometry / D'WALL Specification Catalogue

## Overview
The catalogue is structured as a high-end architectural specification document, organizing the unified D'WALL platform into 10 structured sections.

---

## Structure Breakdown

### 01 — COVER & SYSTEM IDENTITY
- Cover imagery featuring finished D'WALL installation with neat cavity access.
- Brand identity: Jometry / D'WALL Architectural Systems by MACR MAYR India Pvt Ltd.
- Core statement: Modular, demountable, serviceable wall systems.

### 02 — BRAND & SYSTEM INTRODUCTION
- The core problem in commercial architecture: drywall is a one-way decision.
- Comparison: Conventional Drywall vs D'WALL Service Wall (Days vs Minutes, dust vs clean, destroy vs clip).
- Philosophy: "Buildings change. Walls don't." / "The wall that opens."

### 03 — PRODUCT PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE
- Common platform logic: 500 × 500 mm module grid, concealed backer plate, patented toolless mounting node.
- Anatomy of the Cavity: Standoff adjustment, cable rails, water management, air path.
- Access in three moves: 01 Release, 02 Open/Remove, 03 Close/Replace.

### 04 — PRODUCT FAMILIES OVERVIEW
- High-level overview of the 3 primary product lines:
  1. D'WALL Service Wall (Production)
  2. D'WALL Acoustic Panel System (Production)
  3. D'WALL Living Wall System (In Development / Concept)

### 05 — VARIANT CATALOGUE & SPECIFICATION CARDS
- Detailed specification sheets for each variant across the 3 families.
- Standardized specification layout:
  - Product Name & Status Badge (Production vs In Development)
  - Purpose & Key Characteristics
  - Face Geometry & Material Options
  - Technical Parameters & Limitations
  - Integrated Services / Outlets / Accessories

### 06 — APPLICATIONS & SELECTION GUIDES
- Room-by-room application matrix:
  - Healthcare & Clean Rooms

  - Offices & Co-working
  - Retail, Hospitality & Public Transit
