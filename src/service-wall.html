Section 04 · Cavity Engineering

02 Anatomy of the Continuous Cavity

Zone-Configured Standoff Depth: 50 mm to 220 mm

Panels stand clear of the substrate on adjustable standoffs, creating a continuous, ventilated raceway behind the finished elevation. This cavity organizes power, high-speed data, HVAC distribution, and acoustic absorption into isolated rails rather than burying them haphazardly inside studs.

Multi-Tier Raceway Segregation Architecture:

TIER 01 · POWER (230V Mains & UPS)

Grounded metallic raceway isolating high-voltage lines from data interference with continuous EMI barrier.

Universal Frame-Free Grid Mounting

The identical cold-rolled aluminum sub-frame rail mounts acoustic fluted tiles, living bio-cassettes, and illuminated panels interchangeably on the same node geometry.
