Section 01 · Brand & System Introduction

01 Platform Philosophy: Buildings change. Walls don't.

The Modular Platform Principle

Conventional drywall is a permanent, one-way decision. Everything behind it is sealed in.

Reconfiguring cabling, chasing faults, or relocating MEP currently forces wall sawing, dust containment, 48-hour plaster drying, and repainting. JOMETRY transforms the wall into an active, precision-engineered platform with toolless cavity access in under 3 minutes.

Operational Continuity: In corporate headquarters and critical facilities, conventional alterations cause days of downtime and costly permits. JOMETRY allows facilities teams to execute alterations during regular working hours with zero disruption.

Cleanroom & Clinical Safety: Zero airborne gypsum or silica dust. Fully compliant for sterile healthcare suites, operating theatres, diagnostic labs, and ISO Class 5 cleanrooms.

15+ Year Capex Value: Eliminates recurring wall demolition and skimming expenses over building lifecycles. Panels remain 100% reusable and reconfigurable.

Conventional Drywall Partition

  1. Erect plastic dust tent & isolate room
  2. Saw cut drywall board & pull cabling
  3. Patch backing, tape joints & skim wet plaster (24–48h cure)
  4. Sand smooth (silica dust), repaint entire elevation & clean
