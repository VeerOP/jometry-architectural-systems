Section 02 · Operational Assessment

2.0The same job, two ways

Adding a data outlet or repairing services in an existing wall elevation.

Conventional Drywall

  1. Isolate area & erect dust containment tent
  2. Cut open the gypsum board with saw
  3. Pull new cabling / chase electrical fault
  4. Patch backing board, tape and skim plaster
  5. Wait 24–48 hours curing and drying time
  6. Sand smooth — generates airborne silica dust
  7. Repaint entire wall elevation for color match
  8. Clean down and reinstate space
3 to 5 Days Disruption · Room Out of Service

D'WALL Service Wall

  1. Toolless unclip of targeted 500×500 mm cassette
  2. Route new cabling through segregated raceway
  3. Clip cassette firmly back onto concealed node
Under 3 Minutes · Zero Dust · Zero Trades · Zero Downtime

Section 03 · Toolless Operation

3.0Access in three moves

Repeatable toolless engagement that leaves zero trace of maintenance works.

STEP 01 — RELEASE

Press & Disengage

Technician applies calibrated inward pressure to release concealed spring clips without face tools.

STEP 02 — OPEN

Lift Straight Off

Panel lifts off parallel to the elevation, exposing the full cavity width with segregated raceways.

STEP 03 — CLOSE

Snap & Self-Index

Panel snaps flush into the grid. Sub-millimeter alignment is retained by conical indexing nodes.

Key Mechanical Attributes

Section 04 · Cavity Engineering

4.0Anatomy of the cavity

Panels stand clear of the substrate on adjustable standoffs, creating a continuous, ventilated raceway behind the finished elevation.

This cavity organizes power, high-speed data, HVAC distribution, and acoustic absorption into isolated rails rather than burying them haphazardly inside studs.

Zone-Configured Cavity Depth

Standoff depth is specified per architectural zone: 50 mm for power/data lines, 100–180 mm for deep acoustic Helmholtz resonators, ventilation ducts, and irrigation manifolds.

Universal Sub-frame Grid

The identical aluminum rail mounts acoustic fluted tiles, living bio-cassettes, and illuminated panels interchangeably.
