Active Air Biofiltration · Root Rhizosphere Fluid Dynamics

01 Active Root-Zone Air Purification

Active Engineered Service

Root Rhizosphere Dynamic Particle Flow

● Ambient Room Air (VOCs) ● Rhizosphere Microbial Biofilter ● Purified Oxygen Exhaust
Air Exchange: 240 m³/h  ·  Acoustic Emission: 21 dB (Whisper Silent)  ·  VOC Purification: Up to 85% Formaldehyde

Air passes through the living root medium, not simply across outer foliage.

In conventional green walls, air only touches the leaf surface. In JOMETRY's active biofiltration configuration, ambient room air is drawn into the negative-pressure cavity directly through the inorganic wicking matrix and root rhizosphere.

The beneficial symbiotic microbes residing on plant roots break down hazardous VOC pollutants (formaldehyde, benzene, toluene) and particulate matter, converting them into non-toxic plant nourishment.
