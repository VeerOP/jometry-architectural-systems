Section 01 · Brand & System Introduction

01 Platform Philosophy: Buildings change. Walls don't.

The Modular Platform Principle

A drywall partition is a one-way decision. Everything placed behind it is permanently sealed in.

Add a workstation, move a server rack, pull a new data run, chase an electrical fault, or upgrade IT cabling and the conventional wall must be cut open, contained, rebuilt, skimmed, sanded, and repainted. Dust, noise, fumes, drying time, and rooms out of service for days.

In an occupied corporate headquarters or hospital, that costs time twice: once in the works order, and again in the lost operational hours the space cannot be used.

The Modular Platform Principle

JOMETRY unites aesthetic surface cladding with accessible MEP infrastructure. Patented toolless nodes and continuous raceway cavities allow any tile to be removed and re-clipped in under 3 minutes without tools.

Section 02 · Engineering Comparison

03 The same job, two ways

Adding a data outlet or repairing an internal line in an existing wall elevation
Traditional Wet Method

Conventional Drywall Partition

  1. Isolate area & erect plastic dust containment tent
  2. Cut open board with power drywall saw
  3. Pull new cabling / chase electrical fault
  4. Patch backing board, tape joints, skim wet plaster
  5. Wait 24–48 hours curing and drying time
  6. Sand smooth — generating fine airborne silica dust
  7. Repaint entire wall elevation for color match
  8. Clean down and reinstate occupied space
⏱ 3 to 5 Days Disruption · Room Out of Service · Heavy Dust & Fumes
JOMETRY Mechanical Method

WallClick Node Demountable Platform

  1. Tool-less unclip of targeted Panel Surface
  2. Route new cabling through segregated raceway / Change of Function
  3. Clip cassette firmly back onto concealed node
⏱ Under 3 Minutes · Zero Dust · Zero Trades · Zero Downtime
