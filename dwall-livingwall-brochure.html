1.0Why living walls fail

Living walls die. Not from bad botanical selection, but from impossible on-site maintenance.

Traditional living walls are installed as permanent wet fixtures. When individual plants fail or root rot occurs, technicians must garden at height from scaffolding, disturbing building occupants, creating water mess, and staining expensive adjacent finishes in marble, timber, and glass.

The industry's response has been to sell expensive recurring maintenance contracts, or to settle for artificial plastic greenery that adds zero environmental value.

The D'WALL Engineering Reversal

D'WALL treats the living elevation as a modular building service. Pre-cultivated cassettes are established in nursery conditions, slotted toollessly into place, and exchanged in minutes when tired.

Corridor Installation
Continuous Bio-Wall Elevation Corporate Atrium Application

Exchange Over Gardening

Cassettes are grown off-site. Maintenance is an exchange cycle without scissor lifts or dirt on site.

Root-Zone Aeration

Continuous cavity negative pressure pulls ambient air through the roots, preventing mold and stagnation.

Substrate Isolation

Water lines and drainage manifolds are housed in the cavity, 100% segregated from structural drywall.

2.0The modular cassette system

Indoor Living Wall Cladding
Uniform Shadow-Gap Grid Precision Indoor Modular Living Wall Cladding · Mumbai Installation

A precision 500 × 500 mm cassette grid mounted on a concealed sub-frame, forming a continuous ventilated cavity.

Each cassette carries its own growing matrix and established foliage. Each detaches independently without affecting structural integrity or visual alignment of surrounding modules.

The system establishes a monolithic architectural surface with crisp 8–12 mm shadow gaps, no exposed fasteners, and permanent access to building services behind.

The identical sub-frame accepts living biofilter cassettes, microgreen wellness trays, preserved acoustic moss, and perforated air return registers interchangeably.

4.0Cavity architecture & water defence

Water delivery and drainage are contained entirely within the concealed cavity, completely isolated from the architectural substrate.

Excess moisture is channelled internally to a base manifold, preventing staining of lower modules or adjacent finishes in stone, glass, or timber.

4 Independent Layers of Leak Defence

  1. Impermeable Body: Recycled polymer shell with waterproof rear seal.
  2. Internal Drip Channel: Continuous tray draining surplus moisture.
  3. Smart Leak Sensors: Automated shut-off solenoid upon anomaly detection.
  4. Dedicated Manifold: Direct discharge to plumbing — zero slab contact.

Continuous Cavity Ventilation

Constant airflow keeps the back of each cassette dry, eliminating anaerobic stagnation, mold, and mildew.

5.0Active air purification & biofiltration

Active Biofiltration
Root-Zone Micro-Ecosystem Active Air Path Flow

Room air is drawn directly through the living root zone into the negative-pressure cavity raceway.

Beneficial rhizosphere microorganisms metabolise airborne VOCs (formaldehyde, benzene, toluene) and capture fine particulate matter (PM2.5 / PM10).

Unlike passive indoor plants, active biofiltration forces air volume through the dense root matrix where 90% of biological air purification takes place.
